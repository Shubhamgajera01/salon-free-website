
# Real Business Data Required Before Launch

Replace every placeholder before publishing:

- Exact Google Business Profile name
- Real public address
- Real phone number
- Real WhatsApp number
- Opening hours
- Real domain
- Approved salon logo
- Approved salon photographs
- Instagram/Facebook URLs
- Final service list
- Final prices
- Approved customer reviews

## Google presence

The website does not automatically create or manage a Google Business Profile.

After the domain and business details are finalized:
1. Claim/verify the salon's Google Business Profile.
2. Add the official website URL.
3. Keep name, address and phone consistent.
4. Add real business photos.
5. Ask genuine customers for reviews.
6. Use the website's sitemap in Search Console.

Never publish invented address, phone, reviews or business claims.
