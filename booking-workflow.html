PHASE 6

Appointment workflow

The frontend now collects a structured appointment request and prepares customer-ready communication. No private business contact details are hardcoded.

1
Customer selects service

Audience → service → date → time.

2
Customer enters details

Name, mobile number, optional email and notes are validated in the browser.

3
Request is generated

A booking ID is created and the request is stored locally for the current demo device.

4
Owner communication

The customer can send the prepared request through WhatsApp or email. Production deployment should connect these actions to the verified salon number/email or a backend.
